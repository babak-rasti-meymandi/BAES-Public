# BAES — Babak AI Engineering Standard

**Public Engineering Record**

BAES (Babak AI Engineering Standard) is a technology-neutral engineering standard under development for **Human–AI Engineering Interaction**.

BAES focuses on the engineering boundaries between:

* Human Intent
* Authority and Delegation
* AI Reasoning and Investigation
* Evidence
* Recommendation and Challenge
* Human Decision
* Authorized Execution
* Traceability
* Governance

The purpose of this repository is to provide a concise, public-safe introduction to BAES, its current formulation, its potential research relevance, and its creator.

## The Engineering Problem

As AI systems become capable of sustained investigation, reasoning, tool use, recommendation, delegation, and autonomous or semi-autonomous execution, several concepts that are traditionally separated can become tightly coupled within one technical system.

In particular, **technical capability, delegated authority, human decision, and authorized execution are not necessarily the same thing**.

BAES explores whether these distinctions can be represented explicitly enough to remain understandable, traceable, reviewable, and governable as AI-enabled systems become more capable.

This is an engineering problem with potential relevance to Human–AI Interaction, agentic systems, software and systems engineering, AI assurance, authorization, provenance, socio-technical systems, and related fields.

BAES does not claim that its current formulation is the final answer to this problem. The public record documents the development of the proposed engineering structure and provides a basis for further examination.

## What BAES Is

BAES addresses the engineering relationships that arise when AI systems move beyond simple information retrieval and participate in investigation, reasoning, recommendation, decision support, delegation, or execution.

BAES is designed to remain neutral with respect to:

* vendors;
* AI models;
* programming languages;
* frameworks;
* operating systems;
* infrastructure; and
* implementation tools.

BAES is not a software product, AI framework, project-management methodology, software architecture, quality-management system, regulatory framework, or replacement for domain-specific governance.

## The Central Interaction

A simplified conceptual representation is:

**Human Intent → Delegation → AI Investigation / Reasoning → Evidence → Recommendation / Challenge → Human Decision → Authorized Execution → Result → Traceability**

This is a conceptual model, not a mandatory software workflow.

The purpose of the model is to make distinctions and relationships explicit enough to be examined across different implementations and domains.

## Why It May Be Research-Worthy

The development of increasingly capable AI systems raises questions about how human intent, delegated authority, AI reasoning, evidence, human decisions, and execution should be represented and distinguished within engineering systems.

BAES provides one proposed structure for examining these relationships.

Potential research can therefore investigate questions such as:

* whether the proposed distinctions are sufficiently precise;
* whether they remain stable across different AI architectures and domains;
* whether they can be represented in practical engineering systems;
* whether they improve traceability and reviewability;
* where the model fails or requires refinement; and
* how the proposed structure relates to existing engineering, assurance, authorization, and Human–AI Interaction research.

These are open questions rather than claims of established results.
